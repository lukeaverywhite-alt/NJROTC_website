Lesson overview

Uniform Inspection Checks

Use a final head-to-toe routine to find common uniform issues before formation.

Learning objectives

  • Verify the uniform of the day.
  • Check grooming, placement, fasteners, gig line, lint, and shoes.
  • Correct problems before reporting.

Prerequisites / required items

Complete assigned uniform, mirror, ruler, and lint remover.
