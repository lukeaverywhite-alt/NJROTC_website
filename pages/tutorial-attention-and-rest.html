Lesson overview

Attention and Rest Positions

Practice attention, parade rest, at ease, and rest exactly as demonstrated by an instructor.

Learning objectives

  • Recognize the command and permitted movement for each position.
  • Move with control on the command of execution.
  • Maintain bearing until another command is given.

Prerequisites / required items

Clear practice space, safe footwear, and instructor supervision.
