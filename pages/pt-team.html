PT Team

Bring energy, determination, and teamwork to physical events at drill meets.

How it works

Step forward and compete

Cadets who are interested generally ask to join, commonly by sending an email to unit leadership. The team represents Bethel NJROTC in physical events held at drill meets.

  • Participation begins by expressing interest.
  • The unit does not normally hold separate PT Team practices.
  • Cadets may earn medals through drill-meet competition.

Event formats and awards are set by each meet. Current meet instructions take precedence.
