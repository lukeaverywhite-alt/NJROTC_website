Advancement resources

Follow current instructor direction and use the unit promotion sheet as your primary study material for promotion. Find a copy at the front of the NJROTC classroom, by the door next to the staff room. It contains promotion guidance, the questions you may be asked during the promotion process, and basic knowledge drawn from the Cadet Reference Manual.

For additional background, review advancement and qualifications in the Cadet Reference Manual. The archived manual does not replace current instructor direction or the unit promotion sheet.
