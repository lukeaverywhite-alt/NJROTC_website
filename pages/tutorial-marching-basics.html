Lesson overview

Marching Basics

Practice stepping off, cadence, alignment, and halting as part of a formation.

Learning objectives

  • Step off on the correct foot.
  • Maintain cadence, interval, distance, and alignment.
  • Halt under control on the command of execution.

Prerequisites / required items

Instructor-led formation, safe footwear, and a clear drill area.
