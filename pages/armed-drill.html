Armed Drill

Armed Drill combines regulation formations and marching with approved manual-of-arms movements.

Manual of arms

Armed Drill in practice

Bethel cadets train with the unit’s demilitarized M1903 Springfield drill rifles. Movements include right shoulder arms and left shoulder arms, using the terminology and procedures in controlling Navy drill guidance. Training prioritizes command execution, uniform timing, muzzle awareness, and safe handling; event rules determine the routine.
