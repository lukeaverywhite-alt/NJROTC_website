Basic Leadership Training

A fast-paced introduction to teamwork, discipline, and life in the Bethel NJROTC unit.

The BLT experience

Start together. Learn together.

Basic Leadership Training (BLT) brings the unit together with a special focus on incoming Naval Science 1 cadets and freshmen. It adapts the urgency and structure of a traditional military boot camp into a safe, age-appropriate high-school experience where new cadets learn to listen, respond, and work as one team.

From the moment cadets step off the bus—or report at Bethel High School—the pace is intentionally high. Cadet aides issue quick instructions, call for motivation, and may direct exercises such as push-ups. The goal is not individual performance alone: it is learning to stay composed, help shipmates, and accomplish a task under pressure.

A day at BLT

Training through action

Activities may vary by year, but each station develops a skill the unit relies on.

01 / Fundamentals

Drill & discipline

Cadets rotate through stations to learn basic drill, follow commands, and practice military bearing. Even lunch reinforces attention to detail, with cadets seated and eating at attention before returning to training.

02 / Teamwork

Shared challenges

Past events have included an obstacle course and a team Humvee pull. Cadets use cadence, communication, and coordinated effort to keep everyone moving toward the same objective.

03 / Recognition

Finish as a unit

An awards ceremony closes the day and recognizes what cadets accomplished together. The lessons in motivation, resilience, and collaboration continue throughout the school year.

Unit tradition

Where BLT has trained

BLT has changed locations across the unit’s history. Earlier classes trained at West Point, and for many years the program was held at the Danbury Army Reserve Center. More recently, Bethel High School itself has become the training ground.

The setting may change, but the purpose remains consistent: welcome new cadets, build connections across the unit, and establish the habits needed for a successful year.

Required milestone

Basic Leadership ribbon

BLT is a required part of the Bethel NJROTC experience and is necessary to earn the Basic Leadership ribbon. The ribbon is yellow with a red stripe through its center.

Cadets are expected to complete the program as NS1s. A cadet who misses BLT during the first year returns to complete it as an NS2, reflecting how important this foundation is to the unit.

Ribbon reference: Cadet Reference Manual, Third Edition. Current unit instructions determine attendance details and award eligibility.
