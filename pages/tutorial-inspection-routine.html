Lesson overview

Personal Inspection Routine

Build a repeatable preparation routine for uniform, grooming, required knowledge, and bearing.

Learning objectives

  • Read the current inspection instructions.
  • Prepare and check required items in a consistent order.
  • Leave time to correct deficiencies before formation.

Prerequisites / required items

Uniform of the day, Plan of the Week, mirror, ruler, and required study material.

Use current direction: Current instructor guidance, the Plan of the Week, and current governing guidance take precedence over these lesson notes and archived reference material.
