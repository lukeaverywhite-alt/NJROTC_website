Cadet grade structure

The manual organizes cadet grades into enlisted and officer groups. Enlisted grades progress from Cadet Seaman Recruit through petty officer and chief petty officer grades. Officer grades progress from Cadet Ensign through Cadet Captain. Grade identifies a level of responsibility; a billet identifies the job a cadet is currently assigned.

Cadet enlisted grades

  • Cadet Seaman Recruit, Cadet Seaman Apprentice, and Cadet Seaman
  • Cadet Petty Officer Third Class, Second Class, and First Class
  • Cadet Chief Petty Officer, Senior Chief Petty Officer, and Master Chief Petty Officer

Increasing chevrons and specialty-style symbols distinguish the enlisted grades in the manual’s illustrations. Learn both the full title and the accepted spoken form taught by instructors.

Cadet officer grades

  • Cadet Ensign and Cadet Lieutenant Junior Grade
  • Cadet Lieutenant and Cadet Lieutenant Commander
  • Cadet Commander and Cadet Captain

Bars, oak leaves, and eagles distinguish officer grades. Never infer a person’s billet solely from insignia; consult the current unit chain of command.

Recognize and wear insignia

Study shape, number, orientation, and placement together. Insignia should be clean, matched, aligned, and worn only when authorized. The CRM’s printed diagrams remain available in the PDF; this text page avoids reproducing a low-quality or ambiguous extraction. Current instructor-issued measurements control.
