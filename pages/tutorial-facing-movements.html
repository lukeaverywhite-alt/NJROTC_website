Lesson overview

Facing Movements

Practice right face, left face, and about face with balance and controlled timing.

Learning objectives

  • Identify the direction named by each command.
  • Pivot without hopping or leaning.
  • Finish at attention with correct alignment.

Prerequisites / required items

Clear practice space and prior practice at attention.
