Purpose of recognition

NJROTC awards recognize achievement, service, participation, leadership, conduct, academics, athletics, and other contributions. An award is worn only after it has been properly authorized and entered through the unit’s process.

Award categories

The manual surveys NJROTC ribbons and medals as well as recognition that may be sponsored by civic, patriotic, military, and service organizations. Each award has its own criteria. Similar names or ribbon colors do not make awards interchangeable.

  • Achievement and aptitude
  • Distinguished cadet and exemplary conduct
  • Academic, drill, marksmanship, athletic, and physical-fitness performance
  • Leadership training, recruiting, unit service, and community service
  • Participation in teams, competitions, cruises, or special programs

Authorization and wear

Wear ribbons in the prescribed order of precedence, aligned and centered as currently directed. Devices indicate additional awards or a particular level of recognition; they must match the authorization. Do not add a ribbon or device based only on having participated in an event.

Protect the record

Keep certificates and notify the appropriate cadet staff member or instructor if a record appears incomplete. Instructors determine eligibility, approve awards, resolve precedence questions, and announce how recognition will be presented and worn.
