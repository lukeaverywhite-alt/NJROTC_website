Lesson overview

Grooming

Complete a neat personal-appearance check that permits correct wear of the uniform and headgear.

Learning objectives

  • Check hair and grooming before dressing.
  • Confirm headgear fits and sits correctly.
  • Ask an instructor when current policy differs from archived guidance.

Prerequisites / required items

Mirror, grooming supplies, and assigned headgear.

Use current direction: Current instructor guidance, the Plan of the Week, and current governing guidance take precedence over these lesson notes and archived reference material.
