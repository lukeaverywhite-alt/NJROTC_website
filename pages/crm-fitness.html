Archived Cadet Challenge events

The third-edition CRM describes three events: curl-ups, right-angle push-ups, and a one-mile run/walk. Curl-ups and push-ups are performed for two minutes without cadence. The run/walk is completed on a measured, level one-mile course and recorded to the nearest second.

  • Curl-ups: arms remain crossed with hands on opposite shoulders; elbows reach the thighs and shoulder blades return to the mat.
  • Right-angle push-ups: maintain a straight body and lower until the elbows reach approximately 90 degrees.
  • One-mile run/walk: cover the measured distance as quickly as possible; walking is permitted.

Classification and recognition

The archive classifies each event as Outstanding, Excellent, Good, or Satisfactory according to age and category tables. A cadet must achieve at least Satisfactory in all three events for the manual’s Physical Fitness Ribbon. Category points determine whether the ribbon carries a gold, silver, bronze, or no lamp.

Current Bethel assessments

Bethel’s present seasonal events may differ from this archived Cadet Challenge. The archive contains no plank event. Review the Physical Fitness Assessments page for unit events and the complete transcribed CRM tables.

Train safely

Warm up, use correct form, increase training gradually, hydrate, and report pain, injury, or illness. Follow instructor and medical direction; do not use an archived scoring table as individualized exercise or medical advice.
