Why the uniform matters

The NJROTC uniform identifies the wearer with the Navy and the unit. The manual expects cadets to wear it with pride, keep it clean and serviceable, and avoid mixing uniform articles with civilian clothing unless specifically authorized. The uniform of the day and any local exceptions come from current instructor direction.

Care and general wear

  • Inspect garments before wear; replace missing buttons and repair damage.
  • Keep clothing clean, pressed, and free of lint, stains, and loose threads.
  • Keep shoes clean and polished and wear the prescribed socks or hosiery.
  • Wear headgear squarely and remove it indoors unless directed otherwise.
  • Keep pockets from bulging and do not attach unauthorized ornaments.

Use the diagrams in the archived source when an instructor asks you to study a particular uniform. Placement measurements and authorized combinations must be confirmed against current guidance before use.

Personal appearance

Hair and grooming should present a neat, clean appearance and permit proper wear of required headgear. Cosmetics, jewelry, fingernails, facial hair, and accessories are limited by the applicable standard. Because Navy appearance policy changes over time, the detailed limits printed in this third-edition manual are historical reference rather than a current entitlement or restriction.

Prepare for inspection

  1. Read the Plan of the Week and confirm the assigned uniform.
  2. Lay out every required article before dressing.
  3. Use a ruler for insignia, ribbon, and name-tag placement.
  4. Check fit, gig line, fasteners, shoes, and grooming in a full-length mirror.
  5. Ask an instructor to resolve conflicts between the archive and current policy.
