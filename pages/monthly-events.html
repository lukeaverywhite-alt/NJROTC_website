Monthly Events

Prepare for the unit’s monthly uniform and professional-attire requirements.

Once per month

Uniform Day

Uniform Day is required for every Bethel NJROTC cadet and is held one day each month. Cadets wear the Naval Service Uniform (NSU), are inspected during class, and receive a classwork grade.

Once per month

Dress for Success

Dress for Success is held once per month on a Battalion Formation day. Cadets wear business-casual attire, and participation is a classwork grade.

Current unit instructions determine the specific date and any approved attire details.
