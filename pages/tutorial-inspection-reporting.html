Lesson overview

Reporting for Inspection

Practice reporting, answering, and maintaining military bearing during an inspection.

Learning objectives

  • Approach and report as currently directed.
  • Answer clearly and honestly.
  • Maintain bearing while receiving correction.

Prerequisites / required items

Completed personal inspection routine and instructor-provided reporting format.

Use current direction: Current instructor guidance, the Plan of the Week, and current governing guidance take precedence over these lesson notes and archived reference material.
