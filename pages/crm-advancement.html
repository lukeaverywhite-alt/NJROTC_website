Purpose of advancement

Advancement recognizes a cadet’s growth in knowledge, responsibility, leadership, and service. The manual presents promotion as a progression through increasingly demanding duties—not simply time spent in the program. Cadets should learn the standards for the next grade, demonstrate dependable conduct, and be prepared to accept the work that accompanies greater authority.

Qualification and selection

Promotion depends upon meeting the qualifications established for the position and receiving approval through the unit’s advancement process. Academic effort, military bearing, participation, leadership potential, and performance of assigned duties may all inform a recommendation. A vacancy or organizational need can also affect whether a qualified cadet is placed in a particular billet.

No cadet should treat promotion as automatic. The archived manual supplies a national framework, while the Naval Science instructors determine present Bethel requirements, timing, examinations, boards, and final approval.

Authority follows responsibility

A cadet who advances is expected to set the example, know the chain of command, carry out orders, care for subordinates, and report accurately. Rank is an indication of entrusted responsibility. It is not permission to demean another cadet or exercise authority outside an assigned duty.

How to prepare

  1. Learn the duties and knowledge required for the next grade or billet.
  2. Maintain satisfactory academic standing and dependable attendance.
  3. Demonstrate military bearing, proper uniform wear, and respectful conduct.
  4. Practice leading, following, communicating, and accepting correction.
  5. Ask an instructor which current qualification record, test, or board applies.
