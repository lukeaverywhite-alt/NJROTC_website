Lesson overview

Uniform Assembly

Lay out and assemble the uniform of the day in a dependable order.

Learning objectives

  • Identify every assigned uniform article before dressing.
  • Assemble garments in an order that avoids wrinkles and missed items.
  • Confirm the finished uniform against current instructor direction.

Prerequisites / required items

The assigned uniform articles and the current Plan of the Week.

Use current direction: Current instructor guidance, the Plan of the Week, and current governing guidance take precedence over these lesson notes and archived reference material.
