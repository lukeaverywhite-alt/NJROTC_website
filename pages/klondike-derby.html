Klondike Derby

A winter course where speed, coordination, creativity, and focus all matter.

Company challenge

Four teams against four

Alpha Company and Bravo Company each field four teams. Those eight teams tackle the same multi-stage course in a four-versus-four winter competition.

  1. Sled pull — move the team and sled down the opening stretch.
  2. Fence obstacle — clear the barrier and continue together.
  3. Field run — cross the open field to the build station.
  4. Snowman build — complete the course’s winter construction task.
  5. Return run — race back toward the finish area.
  6. Air-rifle marksmanship — close the event with a focused marksmanship test.

Scoring and award details can change from year to year and are intentionally not listed here.
