Lesson overview

Shoe Care

Clean and polish uniform shoes, then protect the finish until inspection.

Learning objectives

  • Remove surface dirt safely.
  • Apply polish in light, even layers.
  • Inspect edges, laces, and the finished shine.

Prerequisites / required items

Uniform shoes, soft cloth or brush, and instructor-approved shoe polish.
