Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions from cadets, families, and prospective cadets.

Does joining NJROTC create a military service obligation?

No. NJROTC is a citizenship and leadership program and participation does not create a military service obligation.

What activities are represented on this website?

The current repository identifies drill and ceremony, athletics and fitness, academic teams, and community service.

Where can I find the weekly schedule?

Use the Plan of the Week page. Current details will appear there after the unit webmaster publishes an approved plan.

How can I learn about joining?

Talk with your school counselor or visit the NJROTC classroom. Verified official contact details can also be published on the Contact page.
