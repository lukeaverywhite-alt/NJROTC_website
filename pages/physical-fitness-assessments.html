Physical Fitness Assessments

Two checkpoints help cadets measure progress from fall to spring.

Seasonal progression

One year, two assessments

The unit uses related PT assessments with one exercise change between seasons. Fall establishes a starting point; spring provides another opportunity to demonstrate growth and earn medals.

Fall PT assessment

Establish the baseline

  • Curl-ups
  • Push-ups
  • One-mile run

The fall assessment measures performance without a unit medal opportunity.

Spring PT assessment

Demonstrate progress

  • Push-ups
  • Plank
  • One-mile run

Cadets can earn medals during the spring assessment.

The seasonal event combinations and spring medal policy above are Bethel NJROTC unit information. The CRM reference below describes the Cadet Challenge as curl-ups, right-angle push-ups, and a one-mile run/walk; it does not provide a plank standard.
