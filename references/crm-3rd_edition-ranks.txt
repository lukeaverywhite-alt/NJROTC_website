PUBLISHED SCOPE
The web page faithfully paraphrases the archived manual’s treatment of enlisted and officer grade sequences; grade versus billet; recognition and authorized wear. Content is organized and normalized for accessible web reading rather than reproduced as page-layout transcription.

OMISSIONS AND ACCESSIBILITY
Insignia artwork is not reproduced because a clear accessible raster extraction could not be verified; cadets are directed to the source PDF and current instructors.

REVIEW BOUNDARY
The source is an archived national manual. Current instructor direction, the Bethel NJROTC Plan of the Week, and current governing Navy NJROTC guidance take precedence. Recheck these printed pages and any replacement edition before revising the web guide. Unit-specific schedules, assignments, assessments, advancement decisions, and uniform direction must remain in their current authoritative locations.
