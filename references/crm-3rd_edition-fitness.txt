PRINTED PAGE 47 — FITNESS / CADET CHALLENGE
1. Curl-ups: Conducted on a flat, clean surface, preferably with a mat. Cadets begin on their backs, knees up, feet flat about 12 inches from the buttocks. Arms remain crossed, hands on opposite shoulders, elbows close to the chest. A partner holds the feet at the instep. On “ready, go,” the cadet curls up until elbows touch thighs, then lowers until shoulder blades touch the floor or mat. Bouncing is not allowed and fingers must remain touching shoulders. Time: two minutes.

2. Right Angle Push-ups: Cadet begins face down in push-up position with hands under shoulders; fingers straight; legs straight, parallel, and slightly apart; toes supporting the feet. Cadet straightens the arms while keeping back and knees straight, then lowers until elbows form a 90-degree angle and upper arms are parallel to the floor. A partner may mark the 90-degree point with a hand. Time: two minutes.

3. One-Mile Run/Walk: Conducted on a flat, measured one-mile course with designated start and finish. Cadets start standing at “ready, go.” Walking is permitted, but cadets are encouraged to cover the distance in the shortest possible time. Score to the nearest second.

PRINTED PAGE 48 — AWARDS FOR COMPLETING THE CADET CHALLENGE
The Physical Fitness Ribbon is awarded to cadets who attain Satisfactory or better in all three Cadet Challenge events: curl-ups, push-ups, and one-mile run/walk. Gold, silver, or bronze lamp recognition is determined by overall point score. The most recent PRT cycle determines the device worn.

PRINTED PAGE 49 — STEPS FOR FINDING YOUR PHYSICAL FITNESS AWARD
At least Satisfactory is required in all three categories. Assign 4 points for Outstanding, 3 for Excellent, 2 for Good, and 1 for Satisfactory. Add the three category numbers.
12–11: Ribbon with Gold lamp
10–9: Ribbon with Silver lamp
8–6: Ribbon with Bronze lamp
5–3: Ribbon
A below-Satisfactory result in any category means no award.

SCOPE NOTE
