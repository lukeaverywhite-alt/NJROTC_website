PUBLISHED SCOPE
The web page faithfully paraphrases the archived manual’s treatment of parts of a command and command voice; positions and facing; formation vocabulary; marching and unit movement; practice habits. Content is organized and normalized for accessible web reading rather than reproduced as page-layout transcription.

OMISSIONS AND ACCESSIBILITY
Text cannot replace instructor demonstration. Competition procedures and current governing drill guidance are outside this archived summary.

REVIEW BOUNDARY
The source is an archived national manual. Current instructor direction, the Bethel NJROTC Plan of the Week, and current governing Navy NJROTC guidance take precedence. Recheck these printed pages and any replacement edition before revising the web guide. Unit-specific schedules, assignments, assessments, advancement decisions, and uniform direction must remain in their current authoritative locations.
