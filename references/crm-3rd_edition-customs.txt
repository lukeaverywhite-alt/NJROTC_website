PUBLISHED SCOPE
The web page faithfully paraphrases the archived manual’s treatment of courtesy and respect; the hand salute; honors to colors and anthem; reporting and forms of address. Content is organized and normalized for accessible web reading rather than reproduced as page-layout transcription.

OMISSIONS AND ACCESSIBILITY
Procedural details are paraphrased for study and do not replace instructor demonstration.

REVIEW BOUNDARY
The source is an archived national manual. Current instructor direction, the Bethel NJROTC Plan of the Week, and current governing Navy NJROTC guidance take precedence. Recheck these printed pages and any replacement edition before revising the web guide. Unit-specific schedules, assignments, assessments, advancement decisions, and uniform direction must remain in their current authoritative locations.
