Welcome aboard

More than a class.
Skills for life.

NJROTC is a citizenship and leadership program where cadets learn by doing.

Through academics, physical fitness, drill, community service, and team competition, Bethel cadets develop practical skills that last well beyond high school. Participation does not create a military service obligation.
